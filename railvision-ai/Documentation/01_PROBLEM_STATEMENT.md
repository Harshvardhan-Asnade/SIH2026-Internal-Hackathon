# Problem Statement
## SIH1349

Indian Railway stations handle massive daily footfall. Existing CCTV systems are purely reactive, requiring human operators to continuously monitor hundreds of screens. 
This results in delayed responses to crowd surges, petty crimes, and worker negligence. 

**RailVision AI** addresses this by applying intelligent computer vision on top of the existing CCTV infrastructure to provide real-time alerts and actionable intelligence.
